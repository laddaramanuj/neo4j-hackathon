# Safe banking for senior citizens

**Customer Advisory ADV-006 | Version 2.2 | Effective from 15 June 2026**

Our senior customers are the most frequent targets of phone and SMS frauds - not because they are careless, but because fraudsters deliberately use fear ("your account will be blocked", "your pension will stop") and good manners against them. This advisory lists simple habits and the special services available to you.

## 1. Services for customers aged 60 and above

- **Senior Citizen Desk:** your complaints are handled by a dedicated desk, and we will call you back within **4 working hours** of your complaint.
- **Priority counter** with seating at every branch.
- **Doorstep banking** for customers aged 70 and above or with a disability - request it through 1800 5000 111 or your branch.
- **Service in your language:** English, Hindi, Marathi and more. Tell us your preferred language and we will record it.
- **Trusted contact:** you may register a family member whom we can contact if we notice unusual activity.

## 2. Five golden rules

1. The Bank never asks for your OTP, UPI PIN, ATM PIN, CVV or passwords - on a call, SMS, WhatsApp or in person.
2. The Bank never asks you to install AnyDesk, TeamViewer QuickSupport or any other app to update KYC, pension details or a life certificate (see ADV-001).
3. You never enter your UPI PIN to receive money (see ADV-003).
4. A call that frightens or rushes you is a warning sign. Hang up and call **1800 5000 111** yourself.
5. When in doubt, visit your branch or ask a trusted family member before acting.

## 3. Do / Don't

**Do**
- Keep SMS alerts active and read them; report any debit you do not recognise immediately.
- Ask for a lower daily UPI and card limit if you rarely make large payments.
- Keep your phone locked and do not accept "help" from strangers at ATMs.
- Save 1800 5000 111 in your phone as "Sahyadri Bank".

**Don't**
- Don't call numbers from SMS messages about KYC, electricity bills, pension or parcels.
- Don't share your screen, or read out codes shown on your phone, to any caller.
- Don't hand over your debit card, cheque book or phone to anyone claiming to be a bank officer.

## 4. If something goes wrong

1. Block your card and UPI in the app or through 1800 5000 111 - blocking is done immediately.
2. Report the transaction at once. If you were not at fault, reporting within 3 working days of receiving the debit alert keeps your liability at zero (see POL-201).
3. Call **1930** or visit cybercrime.gov.in; a family member can help you file.
4. The Senior Citizen Desk will call you back within 4 working hours and guide you through each step.
