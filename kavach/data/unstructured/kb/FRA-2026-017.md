# FRAUD ALERT FRA-2026-017 - KYC-expiry impersonation calls

**Issued by:** Fraud Risk Management (FRM), Head Office, Pune | **Date:** 16-Sep-2026 | **Severity:** HIGH | **Classification:** INTERNAL - staff only, not for customers | **Review date:** 16-Oct-2026

## 1. What we are seeing

Since 15-Sep-2026 FRM has received reports of calls from a person claiming to be from the "KYC Verification Cell, Sahyadri Bank Head Office". The call follows an SMS saying the customer's KYC has expired and the account will be blocked within 24 hours. The customer is asked to install a screen-sharing app "for video KYC", after which UPI debits are made while the call is still in progress. In some cases the caller also asks for an OTP "to confirm KYC".

## 2. Indicators

- SMS from sender header **VK-SHYKYC** with text similar to: "your Sahyadri Bank KYC has EXPIRED today & a/c will be BLOCKED within 24 hrs. Update immediately, call KYC officer ..."
- Caller number **+91 59749 55543**; caller introduces himself as a KYC officer from Head Office.
- Customer mentions AnyDesk (or another remote-access app) installed at the caller's request, and reading out the app's ID/code.
- One or more UPI debits during the call, often with remarks such as "kyc update".
- Beneficiary UPI ID **rkfashions7096@narmadapay** (Narmada Small Finance Bank).

## 3. Actions for staff

1. Any customer reporting a debit with the above: follow SOP-101 at once - hotlist card, block UPI, de-register device, register the dispute (DSP-2026-#####).
2. Raise the beneficiary lien request the same working day (SOP-103).
3. Escalate to FRM (SOP-102), quoting "FRA-2026-017" in the CRM case and email subject.
4. Customers who report the SMS or call **without** losing money: thank them, confirm that the Bank never does KYC by phone or SMS link, advise them to block the number, and log the report in CRM (category "Suspicious call/SMS report") with the number or header.
5. Before closing any intake, search CRM for the caller number, SMS header and beneficiary UPI ID.
6. Branch staff: if a customer is on a call "with the KYC team" while transacting, pause the transaction and verify.

## 5. Customer messaging

Use the ADV-001 wording: "Sahyadri Bank never does KYC through a phone call or an SMS link, and never asks you to install AnyDesk or any screen-sharing app." Do not share the contents of this alert with customers.
