# Policy on Customer Liability in Unauthorised Electronic Banking Transactions

**POL-201 | Version 5.0 | Approved by the Board of Directors on 21-Mar-2026 | Effective from 01-Apr-2026**

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains how Sahyadri Bank decides a customer's liability when money is debited from their account through an electronic banking transaction they did not authorise. It is framed in line with applicable RBI directions on customer protection.

## 2. Scope

UPI, IMPS, NEFT/RTGS through digital channels, internet and mobile banking, debit and credit cards (card-present and card-not-present), ATM and POS transactions.

## 3. Key terms

- **Working day:** a day on which the Bank's branches in the customer's home state are open.
- **Date informed:** the date on which the Bank's SMS/email alert for the transaction was sent to the customer's registered mobile number or email.
- **Report:** a complaint through any channel in section 9. Each report is acknowledged with a dispute id in the format DSP-2026-#####.

## 4. Zero liability

The customer bears no loss where:
(a) the unauthorised transaction occurred due to fraud, negligence or deficiency on the part of the Bank, whether or not the customer has reported it; or
(b) the fault lies neither with the Bank nor with the customer but elsewhere in the system (a third-party breach), **and** the customer reports it within **3 working days** of being informed of the transaction. This applies only where the fraud is not due to customer negligence.

## 5. Limited liability

Where a third-party breach is reported within **4 to 7 working days** of being informed, the customer's liability is limited to the transaction value or the cap below, whichever is lower:

| Account type | Maximum customer liability |
|---|---|
| Basic Savings Bank Deposit accounts | Rs 5,000 |
| Savings accounts (including Senior Savings); current/overdraft accounts of individuals with limit up to Rs 25 lakh; credit cards with limit up to Rs 5 lakh | Rs 10,000 |
| All other current, cash-credit and overdraft accounts; credit cards with limit above Rs 5 lakh | Rs 25,000 |

## 6. Reporting after 7 working days

Customer liability is decided case by case under this board-approved policy, considering the circumstances of the delay.

## 7. Customer negligence

Where the loss is due to the customer's negligence - for example sharing an OTP, PIN, CVV or password, approving a collect request, or giving a caller remote access to the device - the customer bears the loss until the transaction is reported to the Bank. Any loss occurring after the report is borne by the Bank.

## 8. Shadow credit and decision

Within **10 working days from the date of complaint**, the Bank will either give a shadow (provisional) credit of the disputed amount to the customer's account or communicate its decision on liability. The shadow credit becomes final once liability is settled in the customer's favour. The burden of proving customer liability lies with the Bank.

## 9. How to report (24x7)

- Sahyadri Bank app: Help > Report fraud, which also blocks the card and UPI
- Toll-free helpline **1800 5000 111** - cards are hotlisted immediately on request, via app, IVR or agent
- Email customercare@sahyadribank.example.in, or any branch

Customers are also encouraged to report to **1930** or cybercrime.gov.in.

## 10. Grievance redress

If not satisfied with the Bank's decision, write to the Principal Nodal Officer at nodal.officer@sahyadribank.example.in. The customer may thereafter approach the grievance redress mechanism available under applicable RBI directions.
