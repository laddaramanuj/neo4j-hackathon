# Never install screen-sharing apps for "KYC"

**Customer Advisory ADV-001 | Version 3.1 | Effective from 10 July 2026**

Customers are receiving SMS messages and calls claiming that their Sahyadri Bank KYC has "expired" and that the account will be blocked within hours unless KYC is "updated" immediately. The caller then asks the customer to download a screen-sharing or remote-support app - most often AnyDesk or TeamViewer QuickSupport - "for video KYC". These are genuine IT-support tools, but once installed at a stranger's request they let the caller see everything on your screen, including OTPs, and in some cases operate your phone.

## 1. What Sahyadri Bank will never do

1.1 We will never ask you to complete or "re-verify" KYC over a phone call, or through a link sent by SMS, WhatsApp or email.
1.2 We will never ask you to install any app other than the official Sahyadri Bank app from the official app stores.
1.3 No employee will ever ask for your OTP, UPI PIN, ATM PIN, CVV, MPIN or net banking password.
1.4 We do not block accounts at a few hours' notice for KYC. If your periodic KYC update is due, you will see a notice in the app and at your registered email, and you can complete it at any branch or through Video KYC started from inside the Sahyadri Bank app.

## 2. How the fraud usually works

- An SMS says: "your KYC has EXPIRED, a/c will be BLOCKED within 24 hrs, call KYC officer...".
- The caller claims to be from a "KYC Verification Cell" or "Head Office" and sounds official.
- You are asked to install a screen-sharing app and read out the ID or code it shows.
- You are asked to open your UPI or banking app "to verify", or to share an OTP "to confirm KYC". Money leaves your account while you are still on the call.

## 3. Do / Don't

**Do**
- Check your KYC status only in the Sahyadri Bank app (Profile > KYC status) or at your branch.
- Hang up and call us yourself on **1800 5000 111** (toll-free, 24x7), also printed on the back of your debit card.
- Uninstall any remote-access app you installed at a caller's request, and switch off mobile data and Wi-Fi if a session is still open.

**Don't**
- Don't call numbers given in SMS messages, however urgent they sound.
- Don't share the ID or code displayed in a screen-sharing app.
- Don't share OTPs - an OTP is your signature on a payment.

## 4. If you have already given access or lost money

1. Block your debit card and UPI immediately in the app (Services > Block card / UPI) or through the IVR on 1800 5000 111. Blocking is done immediately on request.
2. Report the unauthorised transaction to the Bank at once. The sooner you report, the lower your liability - see POL-201 (Customer Liability Policy).
3. Call the national cyber-crime helpline **1930** or file a complaint at cybercrime.gov.in.
4. Keep the SMS, the caller's number and any screenshots; our staff will ask for them.
