# Policy on Protection of Senior Citizens and Vulnerable Customers

**POL-202 | Version 2.0 | Approved by the Customer Service Committee of the Board on 21-Mar-2026 | Effective from 01-Apr-2026**

## 1. Purpose

To ensure that customers at greater risk of harm - senior citizens and other vulnerable customers - receive fair treatment, priority service and additional protection against fraud.

## 2. Who is covered

- **Senior citizen:** any customer aged 60 years or above, identified in CBS from the date of birth (including all Senior Savings account holders).
- **Vulnerable customer:** customers with a disability, serious illness, recent bereavement, low literacy or limited digital familiarity, signs of cognitive decline, or those facing financial abuse or coercion. Staff flag vulnerability in CRM with the customer's consent.

## 3. Service commitments

3.1 Complaints are routed to the **Senior Citizen Desk**, which owns the case until closure.
3.2 **Callback within 4 working hours** of any complaint or request for help.
3.3 Priority counters and seating at branches; doorstep banking for customers aged 70 and above or with a disability.
3.4 Communication in the customer's preferred language as recorded in CRM; written replies in simple words, without jargon.
3.5 Plain explanation of the dispute id, TAT dates and next steps at every contact.

## 4. Fraud protection

4.1 Unauthorised-transaction complaints from senior citizens are priority HIGH at minimum and CRITICAL where money is lost. They are handled per SOP-101, with the beneficiary lien requested the same working day (SOP-103).
4.2 Branch staff must watch for signs of coercion: the customer is on a phone call while requesting a large withdrawal or transfer, appears to be coached, shows unusual urgency, or makes a first-time high-value transfer to a new beneficiary. Staff should pause politely, ask open questions and involve the Branch Manager.
4.3 Staff must never ask for an OTP or PIN, or help a customer install any app other than the official Sahyadri Bank app.
4.4 With the customer's consent, a trusted contact may be registered and informed of unusual activity.
4.5 Senior customers may ask for reduced daily UPI and card limits. A later request to raise such limits is processed after a confirmation callback from the Senior Citizen Desk.

## 5. Liability

Customer liability follows POL-201. Staff should help vulnerable customers report promptly and record the "date informed" carefully: for frauds not due to customer negligence, reporting within 3 working days keeps the customer's liability at zero.

## 6. Monitoring

The Senior Citizen Desk reports monthly to the Customer Service Committee on complaints received, callback compliance (4 working hours), TAT compliance and fraud losses.

## 7. Training

All customer-facing staff complete annual training on this policy and on current fraud patterns, including active FRA-series alerts.
