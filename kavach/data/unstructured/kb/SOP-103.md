# SOP-103 - Beneficiary-bank lien and recovery process

**Owner:** Cyber Fraud Desk with FRM | **Effective:** 20-Jun-2026 | **Classification:** Internal

## 1. Purpose

When a customer reports an unauthorised or fraudulent transfer to another bank's account, Sahyadri Bank requests the beneficiary's bank to place a lien/freeze on the receiving account, so that funds still available can be preserved and returned.

## 2. When to raise

- For every dispute under SOP-101 where money went to another account or UPI ID.
- As soon as the dispute id is generated, and in any case the same working day. Funds are often moved onward within hours.

## 3. Identify the beneficiary bank

Use the IFSC for account transfers. For UPI, the handle after "@" identifies the bank:

| UPI handle | Beneficiary bank |
|---|---|
| @sahyadri | Sahyadri Bank (on-us: FRM freezes directly) |
| @narmadapay | Narmada Small Finance Bank |
| @gangaupi | Ganga Gramin Bank |
| @konkanpay | Konkan Mercantile Bank |
| @ivbupi | Indus Valley Bank |
| @wgcbupi | Western Ghats Co-operative Bank |

For handles not listed, check the UPI handle directory on the intranet.

## 4. Raising the request

1. Open "Lien request" from the dispute in CRM. Mandatory: dispute id, txn id, UTR, date-time, amount, beneficiary UPI ID/account, IFSC (if known), NCRP acknowledgement (if available).
2. The request is routed through the inter-bank fraud reporting channel under applicable RBI directions, and emailed to the beneficiary bank's nodal officer from lien.desk@sahyadribank.example.in.
3. Record in the case note: "Benef lien req sent to <bank> on <date-time>".
4. If no acknowledgement is received the same working day, the Cyber Fraud Desk follows up with the beneficiary bank's nodal desk and notes the follow-up.

## 5. Outcomes and customer communication

| Response | Action |
|---|---|
| Lien marked for full amount | Inform the customer; release needs a direction from law enforcement (via NCRP) or a court order |
| Lien marked for part amount | Inform the customer of the amount preserved; the balance is handled under POL-201 |
| Funds already withdrawn or moved | Request details of onward accounts; pass to FRM and to police via NCRP |
| Account is on-us | FRM freezes and reviews the account directly |

Never promise the customer that the full amount will be recovered. Recovery from the beneficiary bank is separate from the bank's liability decision and the shadow credit / decision TAT of 10 working days from the complaint (POL-201).

## 6. Recovery and closure

- When funds are received from the beneficiary bank, credit the customer (or adjust against any shadow credit already given) and record the reversal reference.
- Close the lien item only after credit, or after a final "no funds available" confirmation.
- Repeated lien requests against the same beneficiary account must be flagged to FRM (SOP-102, indicator 6).
