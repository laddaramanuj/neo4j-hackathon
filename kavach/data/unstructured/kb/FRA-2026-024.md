# FRAUD ALERT FRA-2026-024 - Fake refund helplines

**Issued by:** Fraud Risk Management (FRM), Head Office, Pune | **Date:** 22-Sep-2026 | **Severity:** MEDIUM | **Classification:** INTERNAL - staff only, not for customers | **Review date:** 22-Oct-2026

## 1. What we are seeing

Customers wanting a refund from an online shopping or food-delivery app search the internet or social media for the app's customer care, call a number shown there, and are told to approve a UPI collect request or scan a QR code "to receive the refund". Entering the UPI PIN debits the customer's own account. Some customers approve more than one request during the same call.

## 2. Indicators

- Customer called a "customer care" or "refund desk" number found through a search engine, online listing or social-media ad.
- Caller number **+91 55853 61825**, presented as "QuickCart customer care".
- Debit(s) through a UPI collect request or QR scan, with remarks such as "refund", "refund process" or "cashback".
- Customer believed they were receiving money when they entered the PIN.

## 3. Actions for staff

1. Block UPI immediately and register a dispute (DSP-2026-#####) under SOP-101. Record the number the customer called and where they found it.
2. Raise the beneficiary lien request the same working day (SOP-103).
3. Advise the customer to call 1930 or file at cybercrime.gov.in, and record the NCRP acknowledgement number.
4. Liability is assessed under POL-201 (collect request approved with PIN). Communicate the decision within 10 working days from the complaint; do not pre-judge on the call.
5. Escalate to FRM (SOP-102) quoting "FRA-2026-024"; search CRM for the caller number and the beneficiary UPI ID before closing the intake.
6. Educate the customer: "You never enter your UPI PIN to receive money" (ADV-003), and "take customer-care numbers only from the company's own app or website" (ADV-004).
