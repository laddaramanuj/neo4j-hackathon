# SOP-102 - Fraud evidence checklist and escalation to FRM

**Owner:** Fraud Risk Management (FRM), Head Office, Pune | **Applies to:** Cyber Fraud Desk, contact centre, branches | **Effective:** 01-Aug-2026 | **Classification:** Internal

## 1. Purpose

Defines the evidence to be captured for every suspected fraud complaint, and when a case must be escalated to FRM for investigation, blocking and inter-bank action. Read with SOP-101 and SOP-103.

## 2. Evidence checklist (attach to the CRM case)

- [ ] Transaction id(s), UTR/RRN, date-time and amount of each disputed debit
- [ ] Beneficiary UPI ID / account number and IFSC / merchant id
- [ ] Caller number(s), SMS sender header(s) and the exact SMS text (screenshot or forwarded copy)
- [ ] App or channel used by the fraudster (screen-sharing app, APK, WhatsApp number, Telegram handle, website link)
- [ ] Digital banking logs: device id, device binding date, IP, login times (FRM can pull these if not visible to you)
- [ ] Customer declaration (signed at branch, or recorded on the call)
- [ ] NCRP acknowledgement number, if already filed
- [ ] Dispute id (DSP-2026-#####) and card/UPI hotlisting reference

## 3. Escalation indicators

Escalate to FRM when **any** of the following is present:

| # | Indicator | Where to check |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Remote-access session during or just before the debits (AnyDesk, TeamViewer QuickSupport or similar) | Customer statement; app security telemetry |
| 2 | Screen sharing, or the customer read out codes/OTP to a caller | Customer statement |
| 3 | New device registered, SIM swap or MPIN reset shortly before the debits | Device binding log |
| 4 | First-time beneficiary receiving a high amount | Transaction history |
| 5 | Rapid successive debits - several debits within minutes, or the daily limit reached | Transaction history |
| 6 | Same beneficiary (UPI ID/account) appearing in another complaint | CRM search on beneficiary field |
| 7 | Same caller number or SMS header appearing in another complaint | CRM search on contact field; active FRA-series alerts |

Indicators 6 and 7 are the most valuable. Always search CRM for the beneficiary and the caller number before closing the intake, and list any linked complaint ids in the escalation.

## 4. How to escalate

1. In CRM, set category "Suspected cyber fraud" and priority HIGH (CRITICAL for senior citizens with a loss, or for linked complaints). Assign to queue "FRM - Investigations".
2. Email frm.desk@sahyadribank.example.in with the dispute id, indicators found and linked complaint ids. Subject line: `FRM ESC | DSP-2026-##### | indicators 1,4,6`.
3. Escalate on the same working day. Linked complaints (indicators 6 or 7) are escalated immediately.
4. FRM decides on watch-listing the beneficiary or number, issuing a fraud alert (FRA series) and inter-bank reporting.

## 5. Don't

- Don't call the fraudster's number or contact the beneficiary yourself.
- Don't share FRM findings or alert contents with customers.
- Don't hold back the lien request (SOP-103) while waiting for FRM.
