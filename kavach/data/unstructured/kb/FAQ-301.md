# FAQs - Failed UPI payments, ATM cash not dispensed, card declines and charges

**FAQ-301 | Version 3.2 | Effective from 15 July 2026**

These timelines follow the Bank's turnaround-time policy for failed transactions, framed in line with applicable RBI directions. "T" is the day of the transaction; a "working day" is a day your home branch is open.

## A. UPI

**A1. My UPI payment failed but the money was debited. When will I get it back?**
Failed UPI debits are reversed automatically by **T+1 working day** (the end of the next working day). You do not need to complain for the reversal to happen.

**A2. What if it is not reversed by T+1?**
The Bank pays compensation of **Rs 100 per day** for each day of delay beyond T+1. If you do not see the reversal, raise a request in the app (Help > Transactions > Failed UPI) or call 1800 5000 111 and quote the UTR.

**A3. The status says "pending". Should I pay again?**
No. Wait for the final status. Paying again may mean paying twice. Never search the internet for a "UPI helpline" - fake numbers are common (see ADV-004).

**A4. I sent money to the wrong UPI ID.**
This is not a failed transaction. Raise a request and we will contact the beneficiary's bank, but a return needs the recipient's consent.

## B. ATM

**B1. The ATM did not give cash but my account was debited.**
The amount is reversed automatically within **5 working days**, whether you used a Sahyadri Bank ATM or another bank's ATM.

**B2. What if it is not reversed within 5 working days?**
You receive **Rs 100 per day** of delay beyond 5 working days as compensation. Raise a complaint with the date, time, ATM location and amount if you do not see the reversal.

**B3. The ATM gave me less cash than I asked for.**
Report it the same day with the ATM location. It is investigated by our ATM Reconciliation team.

## C. Card declined

**C1. Why was my card declined?**
Common reasons: insufficient balance, wrong PIN, daily limit reached, online/contactless/international use switched off, card blocked, hotlisted or expired, or a merchant or network issue.

**C2. How do I fix it?**
In the app, go to Cards > Manage usage to switch channels on or off and set limits. A hotlisted card cannot be reactivated; request a replacement card.

**C3. My card was declined but I was still debited at a shop or website.**
Such debits are normally reversed automatically. If you do not see the reversal, raise a complaint with Card Services quoting the date, merchant and amount.

## D. Charges

**D1. Are there charges for declined transactions?**
A declined ATM withdrawal due to insufficient balance may attract a charge, as listed in the Schedule of Charges at www.sahyadribank.example.in/charges. Declines caused by a technical failure at the Bank's end are never charged.

**D2. I think I was charged wrongly.**
Raise a request in the app or at your branch. Charges found to be wrongly levied are reversed.

**Need help?** 1800 5000 111 (toll-free, 24x7) | customercare@sahyadribank.example.in
If a debit was not made by you, it is not a "failed transaction" - block your card/UPI immediately and report it (see POL-201).
