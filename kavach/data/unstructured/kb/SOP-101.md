# SOP-101 - Handling complaints of unauthorised electronic transactions

**Owner:** Cyber Fraud Desk | **Applies to:** all customer-facing channels and branches | **Effective:** 01-Jul-2026 | **Classification:** Internal

## 1. Purpose

Every report of a debit the customer did not authorise (UPI, IMPS, NEFT, net banking, debit/credit card, ATM) must be contained, registered and communicated the same way on every channel, in line with POL-201 and applicable RBI directions.

## 2. Intake - ask and record

Ask calmly, without blame; record answers in CRM in the customer's words.
1. Date, time and amount of each debit; channel (UPI / card / net banking / ATM).
2. When the customer first came to know (time of SMS/email alert) - this is the "date informed".
3. Beneficiary details as known: UPI ID, account/IFSC, merchant name.
4. Any call, SMS or message before the debit? Caller number, SMS sender header, name used by caller.
5. Any app installed at someone's request (AnyDesk, TeamViewer QuickSupport, an APK)? Still active?
6. Was an OTP, UPI PIN, card PIN or CVV shared? Collect request approved or QR code scanned?
7. Is the phone/SIM with the customer? Any recent SIM or device change?
8. Preferred language; is the customer a senior citizen (60+)?

## 3. Immediate containment (before ending the interaction)

- Hotlist the debit card **immediately on request** - from the agent screen, or guide the customer through the app or IVR.
- Block UPI for the customer's VPA(s), disable net banking/IMPS and de-register the mobile banking device.
- If a remote-access app is active, ask the customer to switch off data/Wi-Fi and uninstall it.

## 4. Register the dispute

- Create the dispute in CRM; the system issues an id in the format **DSP-2026-#####** (5 digits). Share it by SMS and email and read it out on calls.
- Link all debits of one incident under one dispute, with each txn id / UTR.
- Record "date of complaint" and "date informed".

## 5. Evidence and escalation

Collect evidence per SOP-102 and escalate to FRM wherever an SOP-102 indicator is present.

## 6. Beneficiary lien

Request a lien/freeze on the beneficiary account through the beneficiary's bank the same working day (SOP-103); recovery depends on funds available.

## 7. 1930 / NCRP

Advise every customer to call **1930** and/or file at cybercrime.gov.in promptly; record the NCRP acknowledgement number.

## 8. Shadow credit / decision TAT

Tell the customer that within **10 working days from the complaint** the bank will either give a shadow (provisional) credit or communicate its decision. Record the TAT date in the case note. Do not promise a refund before the decision.

## 9. Liability windows (see POL-201)

| Reported within (working days of being informed) | Customer liability |
|---|---|
| Up to 3 days, fraud not due to customer negligence | Zero |
| 4 to 7 days | Limited - capped as per the board-approved policy |
| Beyond 7 days | Case by case, per board-approved policy |

Where credentials were shared, liability is decided under POL-201 section 7; do not pre-judge on the call.

## 10. Language

Communicate in the customer's preferred language (English, Hindi, Marathi, or Hinglish if the customer writes that way), replying in the language used in chat/email with approved templates. If needed, transfer to a language-skilled colleague.

## 11. Senior citizens

Flag customers aged 60+ as priority, route to the **Senior Citizen Desk** and ensure a callback within **4 working hours**. Minimum priority HIGH; CRITICAL where money is lost.

## 12. Don't

- Don't ask for OTP, PIN, CVV or the full card number.
- Don't tell the customer it is "their fault" or that money "cannot come back".
- Don't close the case until the decision is communicated.
