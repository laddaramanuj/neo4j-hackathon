# Fake customer-care numbers on search results and social media

**Customer Advisory ADV-004 | Version 1.3 | Effective from 14 August 2026**

When an order fails or a refund is late, many of us search the internet for the company's "customer care number". Fraudsters know this. They publish fake helpline numbers on search-result pages, online maps, review sites, social-media pages and paid ads - for banks, shopping sites, food-delivery apps, airlines and courier companies.

## 1. How it works

1. You search for "<company> customer care" or "refund helpline" and call the first number you see.
2. A polite "executive" answers, already knows the usual problems and promises a quick refund.
3. You are asked to approve a UPI collect request, scan a QR code, share an OTP or install a screen-sharing app "to process the refund".
4. Instead of a refund, money is debited from your account - sometimes several times during one call.

We do not publish mobile numbers as helplines. Our staff will never ask you to call them back on a personal mobile number.

## 3. Do / Don't

**Do**
- Take customer-care details only from the company's own app or official website, or from your order confirmation.
- For Sahyadri Bank, use only the contact points listed above.
- Remember that a genuine refund is credited to you. It never needs your UPI PIN, a QR scan or your OTP (see ADV-003).
- Report fake pages or numbers to report.phishing@sahyadribank.example.in.

**Don't**
- Don't trust a number just because it is at the top of the search results or on a page with a company logo.
- Don't call numbers posted in social-media comments or sent to you in direct messages.
- Don't install apps or share your screen with a "customer care" executive.
- Don't share your card number, CVV, expiry date, OTP or UPI PIN with anyone.

## 4. If you have lost money

1. Block your card and UPI immediately in the app or through 1800 5000 111.
2. Report to the Bank at once; you will receive a dispute reference (DSP-2026-#####). Early reporting matters - see POL-201.
3. Call **1930** or file at cybercrime.gov.in and share the acknowledgement number with us.
4. Keep the number you called, the time of the call, screenshots and the UPI transaction ID.
