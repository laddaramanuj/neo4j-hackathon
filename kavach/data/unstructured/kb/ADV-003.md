# You never need your UPI PIN to receive money

**Customer Advisory ADV-003 | Version 2.0 | Effective from 05 June 2026**

UPI makes paying easy - and fraudsters take advantage of that. The single most important rule: **entering your UPI PIN always sends money out of your account. You never need to enter your PIN, scan a QR code or approve a request to receive money.**

## 1. What is a UPI collect request?

A collect request ("request money" or "payment request") is raised by someone else asking you to pay them. It appears in the Sahyadri Bank app under Pending Requests, with the requester's name or UPI ID and an amount. When you tap Pay and enter your UPI PIN, the amount is debited from your account.

Fraudsters give requests harmless-looking notes - "Refund", "Cashback", "Prize", "KYC reward" - hoping you will approve without reading the screen.

## 2. Common tricks

- A "buyer" on an online marketplace says they will pay you for an item and sends a QR code or collect request "to receive the payment".
- A fake "customer care" executive promises a refund for a failed order and asks you to approve a request or scan a QR code "to receive the refund" (see ADV-004).
- A caller says a cashback or lottery amount is waiting and you only need to "enter PIN to accept".
- A QR sticker pasted over a shop's genuine QR code sends your money to someone else.

## 3. Do / Don't

**Do**
- Read every screen before entering your PIN: check the name, UPI ID and amount, and the words "Pay" or "Debit".
- Decline unknown collect requests and report them from the app (Pending Requests > Decline > Block and report).
- Check the payee name shown after scanning a shop's QR code before you pay.
- Set a daily UPI limit that suits you (Services > UPI settings > Set limit).

**Don't**
- Don't enter your UPI PIN to "receive" money, a refund or cashback.
- Don't scan QR codes sent by strangers over WhatsApp, SMS or email.
- Don't share your UPI PIN, OTP or screen with anyone, including people who say they are from the Bank.
- Don't approve a request because the caller says it is "only verification" or "a Re 1 test".

## 4. If you approved a request by mistake

1. Block UPI immediately in the app or by calling **1800 5000 111** (toll-free, 24x7).
2. Report the transaction to the Bank the same day through the app (Help > Report fraud), the helpline or any branch. Keep the UPI transaction ID / UTR, the requester's UPI ID and screenshots. You will receive a dispute reference in the format DSP-2026-#####.
3. Call **1930** or register a complaint at cybercrime.gov.in.
4. The Bank will ask the beneficiary's bank to place a lien/freeze on the receiving account. Recovery depends on funds still being available and cannot be guaranteed.
