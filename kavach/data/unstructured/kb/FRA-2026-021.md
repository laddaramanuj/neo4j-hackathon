# FRAUD ALERT FRA-2026-021 - Part-time "task" job offers

**Issued by:** Fraud Risk Management (FRM), Head Office, Pune | **Date:** 18-Sep-2026 | **Severity:** MEDIUM | **Classification:** INTERNAL - staff only, not for customers | **Review date:** 18-Oct-2026

## 1. What we are seeing

Customers report being approached on WhatsApp with offers of Rs 150 per task for "liking videos", and then being added to a Telegram group run by an "HR" admin. After a few small payouts, they are asked to pay "prepaid task" deposits that grow with each round and are never returned. The payments are made by the customer over one or more days, usually by UPI.

## 2. Indicators

- Customer mentions the Telegram handle **@hr_riya_tasks** ("HR Riya") or a group promising fixed daily earnings.
- Small credits (Rs 150-500) from unknown UPI IDs, followed by growing debits to one beneficiary.
- UPI remarks such as "task", "prepaid", "deposit" or "recharge".
- Beneficiary UPI ID **shivshaktiente1558@konkanpay** (Konkan Mercantile Bank).
- Customer says the "earnings" are frozen until a further fee is paid.

## 3. Actions for staff

1. Register the complaint as suspected cyber fraud and raise a dispute (DSP-2026-#####), listing every payment with its UTR.
2. Raise the beneficiary lien request the same working day (SOP-103).
3. Advise the customer to stop all further payments, to call 1930 or file at cybercrime.gov.in, and to share the NCRP acknowledgement number.
4. These payments are usually made by the customer. Liability is assessed under POL-201; do not promise a refund or shadow credit at intake. Explain that the Bank is pursuing the beneficiary bank and will communicate its decision within 10 working days from the complaint.
5. Escalate to FRM (SOP-102) quoting "FRA-2026-021"; search CRM for the beneficiary UPI ID and the Telegram handle before closing the intake.
6. If a customer says their own account was used to "receive and forward" task money, do not advise further; escalate to FRM immediately.
