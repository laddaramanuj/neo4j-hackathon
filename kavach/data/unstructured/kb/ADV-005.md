# "Earn from home" task offers on WhatsApp and Telegram

**Customer Advisory ADV-005 | Version 1.1 | Effective from 18 April 2026**

Messages offering easy money for simple online "tasks" - liking videos, rating hotels, following pages or writing reviews - are now among the most common frauds reported to banks. They target students, homemakers, retired persons and job seekers alike.

## 1. How the scam works

1. An unknown WhatsApp number sends a message such as "Part-time job, earn Rs 3,000-5,000 daily, just like videos".
2. You are added to a Telegram group run by an "HR" or "mentor", where many members post screenshots of their earnings. Most of these members are not real.
3. You complete a few tasks and receive small payouts (Rs 150-500) to build trust.
4. You are then offered "prepaid" or "merchant" tasks: deposit money first, and the "commission" will be returned along with your deposit.
5. The deposits grow with every round. Your "earnings" show on a website dashboard but cannot be withdrawn until you pay a "tax", "unfreezing fee" or "upgrade charge".
6. Eventually the group and the admin disappear.

## 2. Warning signs

- Unsolicited job offers from unknown numbers.
- Earnings far too high for the effort involved.
- Any request to pay money to get work or to "unlock" your own earnings.
- Payments requested to individuals' or unrelated shops' UPI IDs.
- Pressure such as "complete the task in 30 minutes or lose everything".

## 3. Do / Don't

**Do**
- Verify job offers directly with the company through its official website.
- Talk to a family member before sending money to anyone you met online.
- Report the WhatsApp number or Telegram handle inside those apps and at cybercrime.gov.in.

**Don't**
- Don't pay any "deposit", "registration" or "task" amount to earn money.
- Don't pay to withdraw your own "earnings".
- Don't share your bank details, OTP or screen with group admins.
- Don't let anyone use your bank account to receive or pass on money for them. Lending your account is an offence, and the account can be frozen.

## 4. If you have paid

1. Stop all further payments immediately, even if you are promised a refund.
2. Report to Sahyadri Bank through the app (Help > Report fraud), **1800 5000 111** or your branch, with the UPI IDs you paid, amounts, dates and screenshots of the chat.
3. Call **1930** or file at cybercrime.gov.in, and share the acknowledgement number with the Bank.
4. The Bank will ask the receiving bank to freeze the beneficiary account. Payments you made yourself are examined case by case, and recovery depends on funds still being available.
