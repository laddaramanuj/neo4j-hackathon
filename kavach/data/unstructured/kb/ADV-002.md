# Beware of fake electricity-disconnection SMS messages

**Customer Advisory ADV-002 | Version 1.2 | Effective from 22 May 2026**

Fraudsters are sending SMS and WhatsApp messages that appear to come from your electricity distribution company. The message warns that your power supply "will be disconnected tonight" because last month's bill "was not updated", and asks you to call an "electricity officer" on a mobile number. The aim is to frighten you into acting before you have time to think.

## 1. How the scam works

1. You receive the disconnection warning, usually in the evening, when offices are closed and you cannot easily check.
2. The "officer" says your last payment is not reflecting and asks you to pay a small "bill update" or "verification" fee - often only Rs 10 to Rs 50.
3. To "help" you pay, the caller asks you to install a screen-sharing app such as TeamViewer QuickSupport, or sends an APK file dressed up as a bill-payment app to install outside the app store.
4. Once the app is running, the caller can see your screen, read your OTPs and SMS, and make large payments from your account.
5. If you hesitate, you may get repeated calls to increase the pressure.

## 2. Remember

- Electricity companies do not send disconnection warnings from personal mobile numbers and do not ask you to call an "officer".
- No genuine bill payment needs a screen-sharing app or an APK file.
- Sahyadri Bank will never ask you to install any app to pay a utility bill. Use BillPay in the Sahyadri Bank app, net banking, or the distribution company's own official website or app.

## 3. Do / Don't

**Do**
- Check your bill status on the electricity company's official website or app, or on your last bill.
- Pay only through BillPay in the Sahyadri Bank app, net banking or the company's official channels.
- Keep "Install unknown apps" switched off in your phone settings.
- Send suspicious messages (a screenshot is enough) to report.phishing@sahyadribank.example.in.

**Don't**
- Don't call the number given in the SMS.
- Don't install any app or APK sent through SMS, WhatsApp or a link.
- Don't share OTPs, UPI PIN, card details or the code shown in a screen-sharing app.
- Don't pay a "small fee" to someone who contacted you first.

## 4. If money has been debited

1. Block your debit card and UPI at once in the app (Services > Block card / UPI) or call **1800 5000 111** (toll-free, 24x7). Card hotlisting is done immediately on request.
2. Uninstall the app you installed and put your phone in airplane mode.
3. Report the transaction to the Bank immediately. Early reporting limits your liability - see POL-201.
4. Call the national cyber-crime helpline **1930** or file a complaint at cybercrime.gov.in.
